Demos

Small experiments that run in the browser, most of them built for teaching. Each one opens on its own page; nothing to install.

Color and perception

How well do we name what we see? These turn color vision into quick games.

  • Color to name

    A color fills the screen. Pick its name from three choices.

    Phone version

  • Name to color

    A color name appears. Pick the swatch that matches it.

    Phone version

  • Tetrachromacy test

    Find the square that looks slightly different from the others.

  • Guess the RGB

    Match a target color by mixing red, green and blue with sliders.

Solresol and sound

Solresol is a 19th-century language built entirely from the seven notes of the scale, so every word can be sung, played or painted. More at solresol.xyz.

  • Solresol flashcards

    Hear a syllable and press its key. Each note has its own color and glyph.

  • Songs in Solresol

    A familiar tune is drawn from a songbook of a dozen, from Frère Jacques to Ode to Joy. Play it note by note on keys 1 to 7.

    Other versions: twelve songs, piano or flute, with the Star-Spangled Banner

  • Keyboard piano

    An octave you can play from the computer keyboard, A to J and W to Y.

  • Solresol tones

    A syllable appears. Press its number, 1 to 7, to hear the tone and check your answer.
